method:
  - Add a generous amount of [[olive oil]] to the pot. Add the [[onion]] and let it brown.
  - Add the [[carrots]] and [[celery]] and cook for 4 to 5 minutes.
  - Add half of the [[red wine]] (125 ml) and cook for 2 to 3 minutes so the alcohol evaporates.
  - Add the [[ground beef]], stirring and breaking it apart until it turns grey with no large chunks left.
  - Season with a little [[salt]] and [[pepper]], and cook until the liquid released by the meat evaporates.
  - Add the remaining [[red wine]] and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes to evaporate the alcohol.
  - Add the [[tomato passata]], [[tomato paste]], and [[peeled tomatoes]]. Stir everything together and crush the tomatoes with a wooden spoon.
  - Add the [[hot water]] and simmer over low heat for 4 hours, stirring occasionally; if the sauce looks dry, stir in the [[extra water]].
  - After the 4 hours, adjust the [[salt]] and [[pepper]] to taste. Once the sauce has thickened, stir in the [[milk]] and simmer for another 4 to 5 minutes.

A slow-simmered Italian meat sauce built on a classic soffritto of onion,
celery and carrot, with two rounds of red wine to build depth before the long
tomato simmer. The splash of milk stirred in at the end rounds out the
acidity.

## Storage

- Fridge: up to 4 days in an airtight container.
- Freezer: up to 3 months.

## Tips

- Stir the sauce periodically while it simmers and check that it isn't drying out; top it up with the extra water if needed.
- Break up the ground beef thoroughly in step 4 — no large chunks should remain before the liquid evaporates.
