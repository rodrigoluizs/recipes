method:
  - Heat the [[olive oil]] in a pot at power level 12.
  - Add the [[garlic]] and sauté for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Don't let it brown too much or it will taste bitter.
  - Add the [[white rice]] and sauté for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring to coat every grain in the fat.
  - Add the [[water]] and [[salt]]. Stir just once.
  - Keep the pot uncovered, still at power level 12, until the water comes to a boil.
  - "Once it boils, reduce to power level 10 and leave the pot partially covered."
  - Once the water level drops below the surface of the rice, cover the pot completely.
  - Continue cooking at power level 10 until all the water is absorbed (about 15 to 18 minutes total).
  - Turn off the heat and let the rice rest for 5 minutes, keeping the pot covered.
  - Fluff the grains gently with a fork before serving.

## Storage

- Fridge: up to 4 days in an airtight container.
- Freezer: up to 3 months.

## Tips

- Olive oil gives a richer flavor, while vegetable oil is more neutral. Both work great.
- Avoid stirring the rice after the water starts boiling so the grains stay fluffy.
