Two large crepes (28 cm pan), one per person: thin but with a little body,
soft and with a light oat flavor. The protein comes mostly from the eggs; the
oats add fiber and staying power. They work with both sweet and savory
fillings.

## Tips

- If using rolled oats (Haferflocken) instead of oat flour, weigh the same
  25 g and blend well with the other ingredients.
- Oat batter is a bit more delicate to flip — wait until the edges lift
  properly.
- The batter can rest in the fridge; if it gets too thick, loosen it with a
  splash of milk before cooking.

## Variation: French-style

Swap the oat flour for **25 g of all-purpose flour** and blend for just 15–20
seconds. The crepe comes out smoother, thinner, more elastic and easier to
flip — like the French ones ([750g.com](https://www.750g.com/crepes-nature-r81487.htm)).
