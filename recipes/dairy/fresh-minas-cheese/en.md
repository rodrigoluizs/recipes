method:
  - In a large pot, combine the [[pasteurized whole milk]] and the [[salt (milk)]].
  - "Heat until the milk reaches 27°C (80°F), then stir in the [[calcium chloride]]."
  - "Continue heating until the milk reaches 37°C (98°F), then stir in the [[liquid rennet]]."
  - Stir briefly and turn off the heat. Let it rest undisturbed for 40 to 60 minutes, until the curd sets to a pudding-like consistency.
  - Cut the curd into cubes and sprinkle in the [[salt (curd)]]. Let it rest for 2 hours; the curd will start releasing whey.
  - Transfer the curd into cheese molds and let drain for 2 hours, flipping the cheese halfway through to help the whey drain evenly.
  - Refrigerate for 12 hours before unmolding and serving.

A traditional Brazilian fresh cheese made from scratch with just milk, salt,
calcium chloride and rennet. It has a mild, milky flavor and a soft, slightly
crumbly texture.

## Timing

Beyond the 15 min of active prep and the 45 min curd set, plan for a 2 hr
salted rest, another 2 hr draining in the molds, and 12 hr chilling before the
cheese is ready. Total time from start to finish is about 17 hours, most of it
unattended.

## Storage

- Fridge: up to 5–7 days in an airtight container, submerged in a light brine if possible.

## Tips

- Keep a thermometer handy — the 27°C and 37°C thresholds matter for how the curd sets.
- Don't skip flipping the cheese in the mold; it helps the whey drain evenly from both sides.
