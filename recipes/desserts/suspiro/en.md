Crisp French-meringue suspiros — the classic way to use up the egg whites left
over from a yolk-heavy dessert. With the 10 whites from a
[quindão](../quindao/en.md) this makes a big batch; halve it, or freeze whites
you don't use.

## Tips

- Any trace of fat (yolk, greasy bowl) stops the whites from whipping. Separate
  the eggs carefully.
- Twice the weight of sugar to whites gives dry, crunchy suspiros. For a softer,
  marshmallowy centre, use less.
- A few drops of lemon juice or a pinch of cream of tartar help stabilise the
  foam.
- Low and slow: they should dry out, not colour. Leaving them in the switched-off
  oven overnight makes them extra crisp.
