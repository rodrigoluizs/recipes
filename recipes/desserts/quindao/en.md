method:

  - "Pass the [[egg yolks]] through a sieve into a bowl: this removes the membrane and the strong egg smell."
  - Gently stir in the [[sugar]], [[coconut milk]] and [[shredded coconut]] with a spoon — do not whisk, to avoid beating in air.

A big-format quindim: the classic Brazilian coconut-and-yolk custard baked as
one cake in a ring pan instead of individual cups. Rich, glossy and intensely
coconutty.

## Tips

- Sieving the yolks is the one step worth not skipping — it keeps the custard
  smooth and takes away the eggy smell.
- Mix gently; air bubbles make the baked custard spongy instead of dense.
- The water bath keeps the custard from curdling. Keep the water hot and topped
  up.

This recipe uses only the yolks — save the **10 egg whites** and turn them into
[suspiro (meringue)](../suspiro/en.md).
