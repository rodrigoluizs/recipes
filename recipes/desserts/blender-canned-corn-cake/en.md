method:
  - Drain the [[canned corn]] and use the empty can as the measure for the rest of the ingredients.
  - Grease and flour a bundt (ring) cake pan with the [[margarine]] and [[wheat flour]].
  - Preheat the oven to medium (about 180°C/350°F).
  - In a blender, combine the corn, [[milk]], [[sugar]], [[coarse corn flour]], [[soybean oil]] and [[eggs]]. Blend well until the corn is fully broken down.
  - If you like, stir in the [[shredded coconut]] at this point.
  - Add the [[baking powder]] and pulse the blender 3 times.
  - Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake in a medium oven.
  - Bake for about 40 minutes.
  - Do the toothpick test and check for a medium golden color to confirm it's done.
  - Let the cake cool completely before unmolding.

A classic Brazilian pantry-staple cake: canned corn blended straight into the
batter, with the empty can doing double duty as the measuring cup for
everything else. Soft, moist, and about as low-effort as a homemade cake gets.

## Timing

Beyond the 10 min of prep and 40 min in the oven, let the cake cool
completely (about 30 min) before unmolding — it's fragile while warm.

## Tips

- Don't unmold the cake while it's still warm; it needs to cool fully to hold
  its shape.
- The shredded coconut is optional, but it adds a nice chew against the soft
  crumb.
